# Exercise illustration briefs (for ChatGPT image generation)

One PNG per exercise, dropped into `exercises/<slug>.png`. The app's `ℹ` tooltip
shows it automatically. Muscle labels + programme colours below are pulled straight
from the app, so each image's legend matches the in-app Primary/Secondary list.

## How to use
1. Paste the **MASTER STYLE BLOCK** (section A) once at the top of a ChatGPT image request.
2. Paste **one exercise block** (section C) under it. Generate. Save as the given filename.
3. Keep the same chat so the mannequin stays identical across exercises.

Colours are the app's light-theme programme colours (the sheet background is off-white).

---

## A. MASTER STYLE BLOCK — paste once at the top of every request
```text
BODY ILLUSTRATION STYLE (keep identical across every exercise):
Clean, semi-realistic anatomical fitness illustration, refined medical-textbook aesthetic. One consistent gender-neutral athletic anatomical mannequin: lean muscular proportions, low body fat, symmetrical, long natural limbs, defined shoulders/chest/legs, NOT bodybuilder mass. Render the body in very light warm-gray / off-white tones with thin medium-gray contour outlines and subtle gray tonal shading for dimensionality. Anatomy accurate but simplified — muscles, joints, fingers, face suggested by clean lines and subtle tonal regions, not detailed texture. Mostly flat/vector-like shapes with very subtle gradients. NO strong shadows, glossy skin, skin texture, veins, hair, pores, photographic detail, cartoon or 3D-render look. Identical body model, proportions, line weight, shading and scale in every image.

MUSCLE HIGHLIGHTING: overlay active muscles onto the base anatomy following true muscle shapes (not blobs). Primary = strong saturated shade of the exercise's colour; secondary = much lighter (~25%) tint of the same colour.

CANVAS & LAYOUT (every image): 1600x560 px, solid #F4F3EE background, no transparency, no drop shadow, no rounded corners, edge to edge. Three side-by-side panels:
- LEFT: bold #1E2A38 exercise title; below it "Primary muscles" heading with a filled colour dot + label per primary muscle, then "Secondary muscles" heading with lighter dots + labels; beside the legend one anatomical mannequin (view stated per exercise) with those muscles colour-highlighted.
- MIDDLE: same mannequin in the START position holding the equipment, "Start" label bold underneath.
- RIGHT: same mannequin in the END position, "End" label, slim olive-green (#5F7A4B) curved motion arrow showing the movement path.
Equipment (kettlebell, resistance band + door anchor, or bodyweight) drawn in the same flat gray line style. Clinical, minimal, premium fitness-app instructional feel.
```
